YatraAI is an AI-powered smart destination recommendation system designed exclusively for Indian tourists. The platform addresses the critical gap in travel technology: no existing solution understands the unique cultural, dietary, seasonal, and budgetary preferences of the Indian traveller. By combining collaborative filtering, sentiment analysis of multilingual reviews, and seasonal intelligence, YatraAI delivers hyper-personalised destination recommendations that account for factors like vegetarian food availability, family-friendliness, festival calendars, monsoon patterns, and women’s safety.
India generates over 2 billion domestic trips annually and has a rapidly growing outbound travel market
